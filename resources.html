FAQ / THE SHORT ANSWER

Serious questions deserve clear answers.

A private legal system should be understandable before it is installed. Here is the operating brief.

What exactly stays inside the perimeter?01

Vaultr is designed so retrieval, indexing, and reasoning happen inside the configured local runtime. The deployment profile determines the network boundary; verify the path and inspect the source before rollout.

Can a firm choose how Lex is deployed?02

Yes—the deployment desk models three profiles: local runtime, private network, and air-gapped. The right profile is confirmed against your firm’s environment during onboarding.

How do answers stay auditable?03

Lex pairs its outputs with source spans, matter context, and a visible boundary state. The open architecture gives your team a second way to verify the claims.

Is Vaultr another matter-management system?04

Vaultr is the intelligence layer: review, compare, draft, research, and orchestrate work across the documents already in your matter. It complements the systems your firm already uses.

What happens after a deployment request?05

We start with your practice area, firm size, and preferred deployment profile, then shape a private pilot around the work your team needs to see first.

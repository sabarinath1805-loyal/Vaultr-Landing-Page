02 / SHORT ANSWERS

The privacy brief, in plain language.

This page describes an operating model, not a certification. Validate the claims against your own environment before deployment.

Does Vaultr need hosted matter data?01

The local profile is designed so matter documents and inference remain on firm-managed devices. Choose the profile that matches your environment and validate it with your team.

What happens when a firm needs shared control?02

A private-network profile can keep governance centralized inside an approved route while public cloud paths remain outside the boundary.

How should a security lead review the claims?03

Run the illustrative boundary check, download the local packet, inspect the source, and replace every illustrative assumption with your deployment evidence.

Is this a legal privacy notice?04

No. It is a product operating brief intended to make the architecture legible. Your firm should complete its own legal, security, and privilege review.

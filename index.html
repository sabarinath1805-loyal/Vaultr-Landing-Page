Can a force majeure clause excuse non-performance if the supplier could have mitigated?

✳

Harvey · Matter analysis

Generally, no. Courts often require the affected party to take reasonable steps to avoid or mitigate the event's consequences. If practical alternatives were available, the supplier may struggle to establish that non-performance was truly unavoidable.
